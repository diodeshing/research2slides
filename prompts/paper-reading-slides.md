# Paper Reading Slide Guidance — v2

Present another author's work for a graduate group meeting. Use “the authors / 作者” when attribution matters and keep presenter analysis separate from source claims.

Prefer the `group-meeting` theme. Allow enough explanation for equations, method decomposition, and critical analysis, but keep each slide centered on one conclusion or mechanism. Use original Figure/Table/Equation labels. For result slides, identify which paper claim the evidence tests. Preserve limitations and uncertainty.

Use Chinese primary section labels such as `问题定义`, `核心思想`, `方法原理`, `实验结果`, `批判性分析`, and `核心结论`. An English technical subtitle may remain smaller and visually weaker. The audience should immediately recognize the deck as a Chinese research presentation.
