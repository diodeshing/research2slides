# Own Research Slide Guidance — v2

Present the user's own work for a progress review, defense, or conference talk. Use “we / 我们” only for supported contributions. Keep background compact and emphasize design motivation, contribution boundaries, evidence, limitations, and the supported conclusion.

Prefer the `conference-minimal` theme. Use more whitespace, fewer words, and stronger visual hierarchy than paper-reading mode. Do not hide incomplete experiments or turn a planned result into completed evidence.

Use Chinese primary section labels such as `问题定义`, `研究思路`, `方法原理`, `实验结果`, `局限性`, and `核心结论`. An English technical subtitle may remain smaller and visually weaker. The audience should immediately recognize the deck as a Chinese research presentation.
