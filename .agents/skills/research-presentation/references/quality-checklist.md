# Quality Checklist

## Factual

- Every important number and central claim resolves to an evidence node and source locator.
- Figure/Table/Equation identity matches its caption and paper location.
- Presenter analysis is distinguished from source claims.
- No generated visual is presented as an original experimental result.

## Storyline

- Problem -> Solution -> Evidence is complete.
- Contributions are neither omitted nor duplicated.
- Each slide has one main message and a useful transition.
- The content fits the stated audience and mode.

## Visual

- No text overflow, overlap, clipping, distortion, or unreadably small type.
- Primary visuals are legible at presentation distance.
- Annotations explain why the visual matters.
- Source labels remain visible or are reproduced accurately.

## Delivery

- Speaker notes agree with the slide and include purpose, script, visual guidance, transition, and time.
- Total time is the sum of per-slide estimates and fits any user budget after compression.
- Final delivery includes outline, notes, quality report, data artifacts, assets, previews, PPTX, and PDF when those phases are implemented.

## Repair

- Automatic repairs preserve evidence IDs, citations, source visual references, and reported numbers.
- Evidence-changing findings remain manual and visible in the quality report.
- Rerender affected pages for focused review, then rebuild and reinspect the complete deck.
- Keep the pre-QA Slide Spec when an automatic repair changes the artifact.
